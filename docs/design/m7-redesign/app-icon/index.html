Why concept 1

  • It is the product in one glance: the mosaic of student screens (the class screen is the home of the redesign) and the teacher's pointer on one of them — watch and take control.
  • It survives 16 px: the small master is four blocks with one amber — still readable in a Windows tray/taskbar and a Finder list, where the old icon's three cyan tiles merged into a smear.
  • It carries the design system: indigo = the console accent, amber = the one "lit" tile; the same mark sits in the top bar, on the student lock screen's colour family and on the document icons.
  • Distinct in a Dock full of blue squares thanks to the single warm tile; no letter, so it reads the same in Ukrainian, Russian and English.
  • Runner-up: concept 3 (room plan) is the most original but needs its simplified art down to 4 desks below 48 px.

Document icons (concept 1 family)

One page shape, the app mark, and a type badge + colour band: .lcbak amber (sensitive: carries the key — the same amber as "attention"), .lclab teal, .lcreq violet (outgoing request), .lcgrant green (approval). The extension label is text here; convert to outlines when exporting .ico/.icns (tools/make-icon.py).
